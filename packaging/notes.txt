USBSmart

A macOS app for reading SATA drive health through supported USB docks. Includes a drive sidebar, health summary, SMART attributes and a bundled command-line tool.

Install: drag the app into Applications, open Driver setup, activate and approve the driver. Safely eject mounted volumes before reconnecting the dock.

Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later. SMART reads verified on a VIA Labs VL715 and all four bays of a Sabrent dock with a JMicron JMS561 (152d:1561). Other bridge IDs are listed as candidates. SMART reads do not write to the drive. A self-test runs inside the drive and does not change stored data. macOS still controls ordinary filesystem access.

Version 0.5.0 adds drive self-tests to the command-line tool: usbsmart diskN --selftest short|long|conveyance|abort starts or stops the drive's own background test, and --selftest-status shows progress, the self-test log and the ATA error count. Reports and JSON export include the same self-test data. This version updates the driver, so approve it again if macOS asks and reconnect the dock. Verify the downloads with SHA256SUMS.txt.
