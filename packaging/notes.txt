USBSmart

A macOS app for reading SATA drive health through supported USB docks. Includes a drive sidebar, health summary, SMART attributes and a bundled command-line tool.

Install: drag the app into Applications, open Driver setup, activate and approve the driver. Safely eject mounted volumes before reconnecting the dock.

Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later. SMART reads verified on a VIA Labs VL715 and all four bays of a Sabrent dock with a JMicron JMS561 (152d:1561). Other bridge IDs are listed as candidates. SMART reads do not write to the drive. macOS still controls ordinary filesystem access.

Version 0.3.2 is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. The app and CLI use scoped client entitlements to open the driver. Verify the downloads with SHA256SUMS.txt.
