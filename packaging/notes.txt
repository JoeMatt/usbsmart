USBSmart

A macOS app for reading SATA drive health through supported USB docks. Includes a drive sidebar, health summary, SMART attributes and a bundled command-line tool.

Install: drag the app into Applications, open Driver setup, activate and approve the driver. Safely eject mounted volumes before reconnecting the dock.

Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later. Verified on M2 Max, macOS 27.2.1, with a VIA Labs VL715. Eleven other bridge IDs are untested candidates. UAS and NVMe bridges are not verified. SMART reads do not write to the drive; macOS still controls ordinary filesystem access.

The downloadable files must be Developer ID signed, notarized and stapled. Verify SHA256SUMS.txt before installation.
