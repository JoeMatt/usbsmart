Check USB drive health from the macOS menu bar. Close the main window to keep the SMART monitor running, then click its icon to see drive verdicts, refresh, or reopen a report. USBSmart scans every five minutes by default. You can turn that off in the menu.

- Press Command–Comma to open Settings and driver setup.
- Run the bundled `usbsmart --metrics` command to export SMART readings for Prometheus and Grafana.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later. Drag USBSmart into Applications. For a first install, open Settings, activate the driver, and approve it in System Settings. Eject any mounted volumes before reconnecting your dock.

If you already use 0.5.0, replace the app in Applications. This release uses the same driver. The DMG and app are signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Use `SHA256SUMS.txt` to verify the downloads.
